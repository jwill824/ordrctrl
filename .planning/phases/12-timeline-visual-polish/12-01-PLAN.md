  <action>
    In `backend/prisma/schema.prisma`, make two edits:

    1. In the `NativeTask` model, add these two fields after `isAllDay`:
       - `color   String?`
       - `icon    String?`

    2. In the `OverrideType` enum, add two new values after `TITLE_OVERRIDE`:
       - `COLOR_OVERRIDE`
       - `ICON_OVERRIDE`

    ```
    npx prisma migrate dev --name add_task_color_icon
    ```

    Do NOT run `prisma generate` separately — `migrate dev` runs it automatically.
  </action>
  <verify>
